Contact Hollyhock Lane

By the Hollyhock Lane Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We are always glad to hear from readers, and we read every message ourselves. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to fill in and no account to create.

Write to us

Send your note to garden@hollyhocklane.com. Tell us which guide you were reading and what you were hoping to do, and we will do our best to point you to a clear answer. We aim to reply within two business days.

Spotted a mistake on a page? Our editors welcome corrections at editor@hollyhocklane.com.

What we can and cannot help with

We are a small team that writes free guides, so there are limits to what an email can do.

  • We can point you to the right guide, explain a step more clearly, or fix an error on a page.
  • We cannot diagnose a sick plant from afar or recommend specific products — your local Cooperative Extension office and garden center are far better placed for that.
  • We offer no paid services, and we will never ask you for payment.

A few common questions

Is Hollyhock Lane free?
Entirely. Every guide is free to read, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Which hardiness zone is your advice for?
Our guidance is general and covers a broad range of U.S. climates. The Garden Calendar can be set to your own USDA zone, and your local Cooperative Extension office gives the most precise regional advice.
Can you tell me what's wrong with my plant?
We cannot diagnose from a distance, but your Cooperative Extension office often has a free plant clinic that can — a wonderful and underused resource.
Do you cover electrical or plumbing repairs?
No. We keep to simple, low-risk tasks. Electrical, gas, in-wall plumbing, and structural work should always go to a licensed professional.
