About Hollyhock Lane

By the Hollyhock Lane Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Hollyhock Lane is a friendly, independent journal of cottage gardening and gentle home keeping. It grew from a simple belief: that growing a few flowers and vegetables, keeping a tidy lawn, and tending the small repairs of a home are among life's quiet, lasting pleasures — and that the advice for doing so should be plain, patient, and free.

We write for gardeners and homekeepers of every age, and with older hands especially in mind. We know that bending grows harder and small print grows smaller, so we have made our pages large, calm, and easy to read, and we lean toward the gentler ways of doing things — the raised bed over the stooping row, the deep soak over the daily fuss.

How we write our guides

Every guide is written in plain English and in clear steps, with the safety of the reader always in mind. Our gardening advice is general and suited to a broad range of U.S. climates; because timing and plant choice vary so much by region, we point you again and again to your local Cooperative Extension office, which offers free, expert, region-specific help. Drafts may be prepared with AI assistance and are reviewed and edited by our editorial team before publishing, and every page carries a “Last reviewed” date.

What we do not do

Hollyhock Lane is an independent gardening and home journal. It is free from top to bottom, sells nothing, and offers no services. We keep to simple, low-risk home tasks and never cover electrical, gas, or structural work, which belong to a licensed professional. When we mention a product or a brand, it is only to teach; Hollyhock Lane is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any company mentioned.

A small promise

We make a few quiet promises and keep them. Our advice will always be plain, patient, and safe, with every risk flagged and a professional recommended whenever a job calls for one. Our pages will stay free, large-typed, and free of clutter, with nothing to buy and no one trying to sell you anything. And your visit will be yours alone — we gather nothing about you beyond the text size you choose, which never leaves your own device. These are not grand promises, but they are the ones that matter, and we intend to keep every one.

About the photographs

The photographs here are openly licensed images from public collections, downloaded and kept on our own pages rather than borrowed from across the web. Where a license asks for credit, we name the photographer beneath the picture, and a full list lives in the site's image credits file.

Come and say hello

We love to hear from fellow gardeners — a triumph in your beds, a question that has you stumped, a subject you would like us to cover, or a correction we ought to make. Reader notes genuinely shape what we write next, and there is no happier mail than a photograph of something a reader has grown. Write any time to garden@hollyhocklane.com, and see our Contact page for what to expect.
